SilverSet × HAVELSAN

B Class supplier for HAVELSAN

SilverSet Studios is a B Class supplier for HAVELSAN, one of Türkiye's leading defense and software companies. We bring our Unreal Engine visualisation and real-time 3D expertise to HAVELSAN's training and simulation work.

Technical capabilities

  • Unreal Engine 5 development in C++ and Blueprint
  • Real-time rendering with Nanite and Lumen
  • Vehicle, ship and flight controllers
  • Procedural city, building and terrain generation
  • Camera, sensor and surveillance views
  • AI behaviour and scenario systems
  • Operator interfaces and user screens
  • Performance optimisation for desktop and VR

How we work

  1. Discovery

    We define requirements, users and target hardware together.

  2. Prototype

    We validate the approach quickly with a working scene.

  3. Development

    We build systems and content with regular interim deliveries.

  4. Integration and delivery

    We connect to your existing systems and test on target hardware.

  5. Support

    We provide documentation, user training and maintenance.
